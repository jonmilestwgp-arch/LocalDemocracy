Council Structure Map

Councillors, wards, parishes, parties and personal ties — how they connect.

People CSV columns: Id, Name, Ward, CouncilPosition, Party, PartyPosition, then for each of 4 parishes: ParishN, ParishPositionN, ParishWardN, then ImageUrl. Relationships CSV: Id1, Id2, Relationship. Ward–parish CSV: Ward, Parish, ParishWard (optional). On load, the page tries fetching People.csv, Wards.csv and Relationships.csv from its own folder automatically.

Click any item to filter it out of the diagram. Hollow rings are parish councillors with no local authority seat.

Click any person, ward, parish or party to see its connections and centre the diagram on it. Drag nodes to rearrange; scroll to zoom.
